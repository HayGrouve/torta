# UI inspiration sources for the marketing site

**Use hospitality, confectioner, and coffee-studio sites (not candy-pink bakery templates), found through Siteinspire’s Food & Drink / Cafés filters, Awwwards Food + Minimal, Siiimple, and Landbook’s color + Food & Drinks filters — then copy craft and photography, not WebGL theatrics.**

Ticket: [UI inspiration sources for the marketing site](https://github.com/HayGrouve/torta/issues/2). Map: [Marketing site spec for the sweets business](https://github.com/HayGrouve/torta/issues/1). Brief: clean, professional marketing site for cakes, donuts, and other sweets; subtle animation; interactive sections; not tacky, not childish, not pink.

## Recommended galleries and directories

### Siteinspire — primary for this brief

- **URL:** [siteinspire.com](https://www.siteinspire.com/) · [About](https://www.siteinspire.com/about)
- **What it is:** Self-described as “a showcase of the web’s finest design + talent.” Curated by Daniel Howells; “over 8,000 websites”; “none of the featured sites are sponsored entries.” Organized by style, type, and subject, including “minimal website design” and industry categories from e-commerce onward ([About](https://www.siteinspire.com/about)).
- **How to search this brief:** [Food & Drink](https://www.siteinspire.com/websites?categories=92); [Cafés, Bars & Restaurants](https://www.siteinspire.com/websites?categories=117); [Minimal](https://www.siteinspire.com/websites/category/minimal); [E-Commerce](https://www.siteinspire.com/websites/category/e-commerce) for product-grid craft. Combine Food & Drink with Minimal when the UI allows (the combined slug hit a browser-verify wall on this pass). Skip anything whose screenshot is pastel-pink or cartoon illustration.
- **Caveats:** Live “Visit … website” links are the point; screenshots hide motion. Sign-in is required to collect.

### Awwwards — primary for interaction language, secondary for look

- **URL:** [awwwards.com](https://www.awwwards.com/) · [Winning websites](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/)
- **What it is:** “Discover the best website designs of the world. Awwwards recognizes the talent and effort of the best designers, web developers and digital agencies” ([Winning websites](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/)). Site of the Day, nominees, collections.
- **How to search this brief:** [Food](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/food/), [Restaurant](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/restaurant/), [Bakery](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/bakery/), [Minimal](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/minimal/) (“Less is more is the motto of minimalism”). Prefer Honor Mentions that look like hospitality over Site of the Day WebGL experiments. The Bakery tag includes real bakeries *and* design studios named “Bakery” (e.g. Pixel Bakery, Brain Bakery on that list).
- **Caveats:** Award culture favors maximal motion. The brief wants *subtle* animation — treat SOTD as a motion library, not a visual system. “PROMOTED” rows are ads. `/about/` returned 404.

### Siiimple — primary for “not tacky / not childish”

- **URL:** [siiimple.com](https://siiimple.com/) · [About](https://siiimple.com/about)
- **What it is:** “A hand-curated gallery of minimalist web design, published since 2008.” Selection criteria stated on the about page: “clean typography, generous white space, a restrained color palette, and a purposeful, uncluttered layout.” No automated scrape, no pay-for-placement ([About](https://siiimple.com/about)).
- **How to search this brief:** Browse architecture, editorial, and e-commerce categories for spacing and type. Use food/hospitality entries only when the palette is restrained.
- **Caveats:** Explicitly *minimalist*; will under-index playful pastry photography. Pair with a confectioner’s photography, not with Siiimple’s quietest architecture sites as the whole language.

### Landbook — primary for section patterns + color filter (skip pink)

- **URL:** [land-book.com](https://land-book.com/) · [Food & Drinks industry](https://land-book.com/?industry=food-and-drinks)
- **What it is:** “A curated website design gallery updated daily… Every featured website is reviewed by a human curator.” Browse by “category, page section, platform or color.” Also sells Webflow/Framer templates (affiliate links) and has **Landbook Pro** for “unlimited browsing, filters and an ad-free experience” ([home](https://land-book.com/)).
- **How to search this brief:** Industry `food-and-drinks`. Use the color picker toward cocoa, cream, charcoal, stone — not magenta. Use [page sections](https://land-book.com/) (hero, about, footer, CTA) for the later prototype, not the template marketplace.
- **Caveats:** Pro paywall on filters. Template listings are a different job than inspiration. Homepage fetch of `/about-landbook` was 404; purpose copy is on the homepage.

### Details.so — secondary for micro-interactions (paywalled)

- **URL:** [details.so/inspo](https://www.details.so/inspo)
- **What it is:** “The details behind the world's best websites.” Focus: “hero sections, footers, page transitions, preloaders and the micro-interactions in between.” “Every example is captured from a live production site, tagged by hand.” “There are no concept shots that never shipped” ([inspo](https://www.details.so/inspo)).
- **How to search this brief:** Filter for navigation, galleries, carousels, page transitions. Ignore preloaders that last more than a beat.
- **Caveats:** Observed “You've seen less than 1%” / **Details PRO** wall. Useful after a visual direction is chosen, not for picking a palette.

### CSS Design Awards — secondary peer to Awwwards

- **URL:** [cssdesignawards.com](https://cssdesignawards.com/) · [About](https://cssdesignawards.com/about)
- **What it is:** “A web design & development award platform for digital folk, UI/UX peeps and inspiring leaders of the web.” Scores UI / UX / Innovation; Website of the Day.
- **How to search this brief:** Scan nominees for food/hospitality only; discard high-Innovation scores that mean 3D for its own sake.
- **Caveats:** Same maximal-web bias as Awwwards.

### One Page Love — secondary if the first spec is a long single page

- **URL:** [onepagelove.com](https://onepagelove.com/) · [About](https://onepagelove.com/about)
- **What it is:** “The leading showcase of beautiful one-page websites, templates and resources since 2008.” Defines a one-pager as a single long-scrolling page with no extra About/Team/Services pages. Acquired by Some Studio as of July 2026 ([About](https://onepagelove.com/about)). Also has a [sections](https://onepagelove.com/sections) gallery.
- **How to search this brief:** Informational / landing-page genres. Skip template upsells (Framer ads on the home grid).
- **Caveats:** Conversion architecture for this project is not locked ([map](https://github.com/HayGrouve/torta/issues/1)); do not force a one-pager because this gallery exists.

### Godly / Recent — tertiary; the URL still works, the brand on the page is Recent

- **URL:** [godly.website](https://godly.website/) (same feed as [recent.design](https://recent.design/))
- **What it is (observed):** Title and chrome: “Recent — Design Inspiration.” Filters: All / Web / Interface / Branding / Product / Typography / Motion / Illustration / 3D / Editorial / Print / Packaging. Masonry of shots, not a website-awards list.
- **How to search this brief:** **Web**, **Editorial**, **Typography**, **Motion**. Skip Illustration / 3D / Packaging mascots.
- **Caveats:** [godly.design](https://godly.design/) is a stub (“Welcome to Blume — markdown-first docs”) — **not** the gallery. The live feed mixes app UI, mascots, and WebGL widgets; it is not curated for food. No first-party about page at `/about` (that URL still served the feed).

### Dribbble — tertiary, high false-positive rate for this brief

- **URL:** [dribbble.com/about](https://dribbble.com/about)
- **What it is:** “The world’s destination for design” / “the world’s leading platform for discovering top designers, their work, and the services they offer” ([About](https://dribbble.com/about)).
- **How to search this brief:** If the homepage captcha is cleared, search **confectionery**, **pastry**, **hospitality website**, **restaurant website** — not “bakery” or “cupcake.” Filter to Web Design. Prefer case studies of shipped sites over dribbble-only shots.
- **Caveats:** Homepage required a JS robot check on this pass. The platform is a hiring marketplace of *concepts*; bakery keywords will flood pink frosting mockups. Do not treat a shot as a live site.

### Behance — tertiary, same caveat as Dribbble

- **URL:** [behance.net](https://www.behance.net/) · [Community Guidelines](https://www.behance.net/misc/community)
- **What it is:** “A platform for creative professionals around the world to showcase their own work and to discover the creative work of others” via Search, Live, and Jobs ([Guidelines](https://www.behance.net/misc/community)). Adobe-owned.

- **Caveats:** Homepage timed out here. Concept presentations dominate. “Behance Pro” is a paid layer.

### Dark Mode Design — optional only if a prototype goes dark

- **What it is:** “A showcase of beautifully designed and inspiring dark mode websites… All sites handpicked and curated by Cai Cardenas” ([About](https://www.darkmodedesign.com/about)).

### Httpster — optional for type-forward layouts, not for tone

- **Caveats:** Brutalism is easy to confuse with “professional” and can look cold for sweets. Use for type and grid, not voice.

### Hoverstat.es — optional for *subtle* interaction ideas

## Recommended reference site types

Look at **haute pâtisserie, bean-to-bar chocolate, destination restaurants, and specialty coffee/bakery hospitality** — businesses that sell craft, season, and place. Do not look at cupcake chains, funfetti nostalgia brands, or weekly-flavor cookie franchises.

### Fits the brief (opened)

- **[Pierre Hermé](https://www.pierreherme.com/)** — Luxury confectioner: macarons, chocolates, pâtisserie as named collections, not “cute treats.” Video hero, product families, boutique list. Adult, French, product-led. Watch for house colors (rose-pistachio is brand, not a license to go pink).
- **[Dandelion Chocolate](https://www.dandelionchocolate.com/)** — Bean-to-bar studio: “just two ingredients,” factory/café visit, editorial factory notes, origin story. Cream/paper + photography; professional without being a restaurant clone.
- **[Valrhona](https://www.valrhona.com/en/)** — Chocolate house since 1922, chef-facing. News, school, ranges, sustainability. Good for *B2B gravitas* and restrained food photography; too institutional to copy as a small sweets-business voice.
- **[Noma](https://noma.dk/)** — Destination restaurant as editorial letter + seasons + pantry products. Typography and long-form hospitality. Steal pacing and seriousness, not the fine-dining identity.
- **[Core by Clare Smyth](https://www.corebyclaresmyth.com/)** — “Elegant fine-dining… natural, sustainable food.” Photography of room and produce, short chef quote, menus. Calm professional hospitality.
- **[Gjusta](https://gjusta.com/)** — Venice bakery/grocer. Short, ingredient-driven copy; catering/gifts/jobs. Honest neighborhood bakery without cartoon pastry. Sparse — more IA than motion.
- **[La Cabra](https://www.lacabra.com/)** — Specialty coffee + bakery/café merchandising: seasonal lots, stoneware, apparel. Nordic product grid; good for how to sell *goods* next to *craft* without childish packaging.
- **[RICHART](https://www.richart.com/)** — French chocolate + macarons as aromatic families and jewel-like boxes. Refined product storytelling. Visuals are colorful by product; keep that in photography, not in chrome/UI pink.
- **[Destroyer LA](https://destroyer.la/)** — Culver City café; type-as-interface, hours, address, one program note. Interactive to the point of a game. Fits “interactive sections”; fails if the prototype becomes a toy. Siteinspire lists it under Cafés.

### Mixed / use with a knife

- **[Dominique Ansel](https://www.dominiqueansel.com/)** — Real pastry chef, Cronut®, workshops. Layout is promo-stack + Instagram. Craft is in the product, not the UI. Do not copy the landing-page clutter.
- **[Sunbeam Bagels](https://sunbeambagels.com/)** — Awwwards Food HM. Friendly local bagel shop: “Drop a Bagel. Or 20.” Warm, not pink. Tone is casual-American; too cute as a system, useful for simple section rhythm.
- **[Gucci Osteria](https://www.gucciosteria.com/)** — Luxury hospitality, almost no copy in the fetch (“Italian, contemporary restaurants…”). Fashion-house sheen; too branded to another maison.
- **[Recchiuti](https://www.recchiuti.com/)** — Serious confections (Black Box, pairings). Chrome in the fetch included a purple widget theme (`#574cd5`). Product names are adult; UI chrome is not a model.
- **[Eleven Madison Park](https://www.elevenmadisonpark.com/)** — Fetch returned essentially a name. Likely a heavy client-rendered site; re-open in a real browser before citing layout.
- **[Deadstock Coffee](https://deadstockcoffee.com/)** — Awwwards Food entry. Streetwear/hype coffee (“Coffee should be dope”). Energy is wrong for this brief; motion/drop merchandising might still teach product drops.

### Fails the brief (opened so we can name the anti-pattern)

- **[Crumbl](https://www.crumblcookies.com/)** — Weekly flavors, franchise menu, **“Pink Sugar Cookie”** as a classic. Childish product language and pink as a SKU. Do not moodboard.
- **[Milk Bar](https://milkbarstore.com/)** — “Bite Into A Sweet Summer,” Confetti Cake, “rainbow-flecked,” sleepaway camp, “HEY, NICE TO TREAT YOU.” Nostalgia-childish by design. Strong commerce, wrong tone.
- **[Sprinkles](https://www.sprinkles.com/)** — “Spreading Joy in every bite!”, cupcake ATM, puppy cupcakes, “bla bla bla.” Cartoon celebration brand.
- **[Ladurée](https://www.laduree.fr/)** — Real pâtisserie house, but the live merchandising is pastel gift boxes, Casablanca collab, “L’art du luxe à la française.” Too * palais / macaron-pastel* for a “not pink” brief; easy to slide into the look we are avoiding.
- **[Nine Times Bakery](https://ninetimesbakery.com/)** — On Awwwards Bakery. Shopify-default dietary bakery: testimonials, “No Gluten / No Dairy.” Not tacky-pink, but template-retail, not a visual direction.
- **[maison-du-chocolat.com](https://www.maison-du-chocolat.com/)** — Did **not** resolve to the historic chocolatier; generic chocolate SEO copy and a Valrhona Selection link. Do not treat this URL as a reference.

## Explicit anti-patterns

- **Keyword “bakery” in award galleries** — [Awwwards Bakery](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/bakery/) mixes pastry shops with agencies named Bakery. Filter by the actual domain.
- **Dribbble/Behance “bakery / cupcake / donut” moodboards** — concept shots, frosting palettes, mascots. Platforms are real; that *query* is the trap.
- **Shopify / ThemeForest / Framer “bakery template” aisles** — Landbook and One Page Love sell or promote templates; that is a different artifact than a locked visual direction ([map](https://github.com/HayGrouve/torta/issues/1)).
- **Awwwards Site of the Day as a style guide** — WebGL, loaders, scroll hijacking. Conflicts with “subtle animations.”
- **Httpster brutalism and Hoverstat.es alternative web as the whole brand** — useful for one interaction, wrong as the sweets-business face.
- **Dark-mode galleries as default** — pastry wants light, steam, crumb; darkmode.design was down; darkmodedesign.com is a niche.
- **Candy / weekly-cookie / funfetti / puppy-cupcake commerce** — Crumbl, Milk Bar, Sprinkles as opened.
- **Pastel macaron-palais chrome** — Ladurée as opened; rose as a *product* is fine, rose as the UI is not.
- **Godly.design** — not the gallery.
- **Lapa Ninja until a human load of the homepage succeeds.**

## How a later prototype session should use this list

Ticket [Visual-direction prototypes for the marketing site](https://github.com/HayGrouve/torta/issues/7) should not start on Dribbble.

1. **Collect 8–12 live URLs**, not shots: 3 confectioners (start with Pierre Hermé, Dandelion, RICHART), 2 hospitality editorials (Noma, Core), 2 neighborhood food studios (Gjusta, La Cabra), 2 Siteinspire Food & Drink + Minimal picks opened in a real browser, 1 Awwwards Minimal or Food HM that is *not* WebGL-first.
2. **Steal from different layers:** photography and product naming from confectioners; type, margins, and nav quietness from Siiimple / Siteinspire Minimal; one hover or section transition from Details.so or Hoverstat.es — not all three from Awwwards SOTD.
3. **Palette test:** cream, cocoa, charcoal, fruit stain from photography. If a screenshot is pink, drop it. Landbook color filter is the gallery that states color browsing on the site itself ([home](https://land-book.com/)).
4. **Prototype 2–3 directions** (paper-light confectioner; charcoal hospitality; product-grid coffee-studio) as throwaways, then lock in [Lock visual direction from the prototypes](https://github.com/HayGrouve/torta/issues/8). Stack (TanStack Start, shadcn/ui) is implementation, not a look.
5. **Re-open** any site that timed out or 500’d before putting it on a board.
