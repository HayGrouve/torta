# Sweets marketing site

Glossary for the public marketing site and the sweets business it represents. Implementation details do not belong here.

## Language

**Marketing site**:
The public website that presents the sweets business, written in Bulgarian, at [iliaora.com](https://iliaora.com). No accounts and no stored orders.
_Avoid_: app, platform, shop, bilingual site, iliaora.bg as the public host

**Sweets business**:
The forthcoming offering of cakes, donuts, and other sweets, publicly named Iliaora. It is made to order; there is no shop and nothing sits on a shelf.
_Avoid_: brand, bakery, shop, café, walk-in

**Iliaora**:
The house name, always written in Latin script. It is the public name on the marketing site’s chrome, title, and logo. Instagram is [@iliaora_bakery](https://www.instagram.com/iliaora_bakery/); “bakery” is not part of the wordmark.
_Avoid_: Torta (repo only), Iliaora Bakery, Илиаора, brand

**Spec**:
The locked description of the marketing site — pages, tone, content, conversion, and visual direction — that this wayfinder map produces.
_Avoid_: live site, production implementation

**Baker**:
Илияна — the person who makes the sweets. She appears on the marketing site as first name only, in Cyrillic, in a byline («сладкиши от Илияна») and as the About heading, not in the header or wordmark.
_Avoid_: owner, user, chef, her legal surname in chrome

**Visitor**:
A person browsing the marketing site. Written first to an adult in Sofia choosing sweets for themselves or a small occasion; second to someone in Bulgaria planning a wedding or large celebration cake.
_Avoid_: user, customer, tourist, wholesale buyer, child as the buyer

**Home market**:
Sofia. Everyday sweets are for visitors who can take them locally.
_Avoid_: nationwide shop, export market

**Occasion cake**:
A wedding or other large celebration cake that may travel in Bulgaria by inquiry, priced per cake.
_Avoid_: everyday sweets shipped nationwide

**Catalog**:
Examples of work on Instagram ([@iliaora_bakery](https://www.instagram.com/iliaora_bakery/)) — what the baker has made, not what is in stock. The marketing site sends the visitor there to look, not to place an order.
_Avoid_: shop, store, menu as checkout, stock list, Instagram DM as the order desk, stills as the catalog

**Still**:
A hosted photograph of the baker’s sweets on the marketing site. There are four silent stills: one full-bleed after the hero, then one each for торти, донъти, and торти за повод. They show the sweets only — not faces, tables, or a portrait of the baker.
_Avoid_: gallery, catalog, stock photo, Instagram embed, captioned photo

**Repertoire**:
The house set the baker already makes: торти, донъти, and торти за повод. Other sweets may be mentioned in passing, not as a named category.
_Avoid_: menu, SKU list, custom orders as a product name, понички, сватбени торти as the only occasion label

**Made to order**:
Every piece is baked after inquiry, from the repertoire. Nothing is held as stock.
_Avoid_: shelf, walk-in, stock, made-ahead

**House line**:
«Нашият десерт по ваш вкус!» — the public catchphrase on the marketing site, under the Iliaora wordmark.
_Avoid_: slogan, English tagline, replacing the byline

**Inquiry**:
A call or Viber message to the baker to order everyday sweets or an occasion cake. Pickup, local drop-off, travel, ingredients, and allergens are arranged or confirmed there. There is no street address on the marketing site.
_Avoid_: form, email, WhatsApp, checkout, public address
